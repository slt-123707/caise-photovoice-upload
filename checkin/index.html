What is this?

During term time, we're asking you to tell us about any moments you noticed in school with AI or technology: something that was useful, something that broke or was difficult, or anything else you noticed.

You can write a few words or record a voice note. If nothing happened, just say so. That still helps.

What happens to what you send

  • It goes straight to the CAISE research team at UCL and is stored in a folder that only the team can see.
  • Your link is just for you. It doesn't contain your name, and nothing you send is visible to anyone else in the study.
  • Voice notes are only listened to by the research team and are written up without your name.
  • If something doesn't send (for example, no wifi), it's kept on this device for up to a week and sent automatically next time you open your link here. After a week it's deleted from the device.

Questions or worries?

Talk to your teacher or the CAISE researcher at your next session, or use the contact details on your information sheet.
